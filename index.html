Suggestions for Sharing

Take time to read through these guidelines before sharing!
1Be open and vulnerable at your own pace.

We encourage you to share honestly, but you never have to share more than you’re comfortable with. What’s shared in the group stays in the group.

2Share the air.

Be mindful of how long you’re talking, and make room for others. If you tend to talk a lot, try to listen more. If you tend to stay quiet, we’d love to hear from you!

3Respond with patience and grace.

Feel free to respond to what others share. You don’t need the perfect answer. Listen well, ask questions, and let this be a real conversation, not just taking turns.

4Listen to understand, not to fix.

Most of the time, people don’t need advice. They need to feel heard. Ask before offering suggestions.

5Speak from your own experience.

Use “I” statements and share your own story rather than speaking for others or generalizing.

6Silence is okay.

Pauses are normal. Give people time to think before jumping in to fill the space.

7Be present.

Put your phone away and give the person sharing your full attention.

8Point each other to Jesus.

As we share, let’s remind each other of God’s faithfulness and lift each other up in prayer.
